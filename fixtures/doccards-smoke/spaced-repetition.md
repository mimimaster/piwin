# Spaced Repetition

Spaced repetition is a review schedule that fights forgetting.
It shows a card again just before you are likely to forget it.

## Why it works

The forgetting curve says memory decays quickly after first learning.
Each successful review flattens the curve and lengthens the next interval.

## Ratings

- Again: you forgot; reset to a short interval.
- Hard: you remembered with effort; grow the interval slowly.
- Good: you remembered comfortably; use the standard interval.
- Easy: it felt trivial; jump to a longer interval.
